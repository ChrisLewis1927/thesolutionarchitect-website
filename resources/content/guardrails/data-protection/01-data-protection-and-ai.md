## Introduction

Using AI with government data raises significant data protection and privacy considerations. The UK GDPR, Data Protection Act 2018, and ICO guidance on AI and data protection provide the legal framework that solution architects must work within.

## Key Considerations

When processing personal data through AI systems, organisations must establish a lawful basis for processing, conduct Data Protection Impact Assessments (DPIAs) for high-risk processing, ensure data minimisation principles are applied, and provide transparency to data subjects about automated decision-making.

## Rights and Safeguards

Under UK GDPR Article 22, individuals have the right not to be subject to solely automated decisions that significantly affect them. Government services using AI must implement meaningful human oversight and provide mechanisms for individuals to challenge automated decisions.

## Practical Application

Solution architects should work closely with Data Protection Officers when designing AI systems. Ensure that training data is appropriately anonymised, that model outputs do not inadvertently reveal personal data, and that data retention policies are applied to AI interaction logs.
