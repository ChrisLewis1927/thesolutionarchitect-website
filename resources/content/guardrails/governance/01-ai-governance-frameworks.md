## Introduction

AI governance in UK government is guided by a set of principles and frameworks designed to ensure that AI systems are used responsibly, transparently, and in the public interest.

The UK government's approach to AI governance draws on the CDEI (Centre for Data Ethics and Innovation) framework, the Alan Turing Institute's guidance, and cross-government policies on algorithmic transparency.

## Key Principles

Government departments deploying AI must adhere to several core principles:

- Transparency and explainability of AI decisions
- Accountability for AI system outcomes
- Fairness and avoidance of bias
- Safety and robustness of AI systems
- Contestability of AI-assisted decisions

## Practical Application

When advising teams on AI adoption, solution architects should ensure governance structures are in place before deployment. This includes establishing an AI ethics board or review process, documenting model decisions in an algorithmic transparency record, and defining clear escalation paths for AI-related incidents.
