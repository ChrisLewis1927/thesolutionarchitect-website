Privacy & Cookies

How this site uses cookies, what data is collected, and your choices.

Who we are

thesolutionarchitect.uk is a personal educational resource for solution architects. It has no commercial purpose and does not sell data.

What cookies we use

This site uses only two cookies, both set by Google Analytics:

  • _ga — Used to distinguish unique visitors. Expires after 2 years.
  • _ga_DF1MYHFXP4 — Used to maintain session state. Expires after 2 years.

These cookies help us understand how visitors use the site (page views, time on page, referral source). They do not identify you personally.

No other cookies are used. There is no advertising, no social media tracking, and no personalisation.

Your choices

You can accept or reject analytics cookies using the banner shown on your first visit. You can change your mind at any time by clicking the button below.

What data Google Analytics collects

When you accept analytics cookies, Google Analytics collects:

  • Anonymised page view data
  • Approximate geographic region (country level)
  • Device type and browser type
  • Referring website

IP addresses are anonymised. No names, emails, or personal data is collected.

Data controller

This is a personal site. No data controller registration is required as it only processes anonymised analytics data.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please contact chris@thesolutionarchitect.uk.

Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. Last updated: June 2026.
